The work

I teach mathematics because I love the subject. I founded MathBridge because I am fascinated by how students come to understand it.

MathBridge is a private mathematics academy for students who already enjoy the subject and want to go further. We teach mathematics as a language, one a student can learn to read, write and think in. Teaching is one to one, and places are few.

My academic life has been shaped by two disciplines. I hold a Bachelor of Science in Mathematical Statistics, earned in South Africa, and a Master of Fine Arts in Creative Writing, completed in the United States as a Fulbright Scholar. Both ask me to interpret closely, express ideas clearly and pursue understanding on purpose. MathBridge grew from that intersection.

School teaches students to answer mathematics. We begin a step earlier, with the definitions, diagrams and proofs they are usually rushed past, and we slow down until each one makes sense. It is the step most teaching skips. It is the one we start with.

Three courses, one path

01

ReadLike a Mathematician

Learning to read mathematics, its notation, definitions and proofs, with real understanding.

02

WriteLike a Mathematician

Learning to write mathematics, so the reasoning is clear and the thinking belongs to the student.

03

ThinkLike a Mathematician

An independent inquiry into a question that interests the student, and original work to show for it.

Other ways in

The Portrait

Two short sittings, and a written account of how a student actually works. No score and no ranking.

The Prologue

The ideas that unsettle Higher Level students most, met in a small group before class arrives at them.

The Practice Room

A set of problems each Monday, and a written commentary each Saturday, so the language stays familiar.
