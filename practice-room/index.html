The Practice Room

A good problem every day of the school year, and a written note each week on how it opens up.

A course ends. The habit should not. The Practice Room is somewhere to keep meeting good mathematics once the teaching is over, or long before it begins.

Five problems a week, Monday to Friday. On Saturday a written commentary on the week, which is not a mark scheme but an account of what the question was really asking, the first attempt that fails, the move that works, and what to carry forward.

Nothing new to learn and nothing to catch up on. Just steady contact with good mathematics, which is how any real skill is kept.
