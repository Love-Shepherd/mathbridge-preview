Admissions

How you begin depends on what you are beginning.

There is no single application. Each way into MathBridge has its own first step, and each one is short. Here is how they work.

To be taught one to one

Begin with The Portrait.

The one to one path begins with a short assessment called The Portrait. Over two sittings it shows how you read mathematics, not how many marks you drop. It ends with a written account of the reader you already are and a clear recommendation for where to start.

Its fee of R1 500 credits in full to any course that follows. If one to one is not the right fit for you now, we will say so, and point you to what is.

Begin with The Portrait
To learn in a group

Reserve a place.

The Prologue runs in small groups a few times a year, for students beginning Higher Level mathematics in the IB Diploma. There is no assessment to sit. A short readiness check confirms the group will suit you. Then you reserve your place, and we hold it until the cohort is full.

See the programme
To stay close every day

Join the Practice Room.

Mathematics is a language, and a language stays alive by being spoken a little, often. The Practice Room is one good problem every weekday and a letter each Saturday, opening in January 2027. When it does, you simply join, at R500 a month. Tell us now if you would like to be first to hear.

One thing in common

Whichever way you begin, we read everything ourselves.

The students who do best here are curious, ready to ask questions and willing to stay with a hard idea. We reply to everyone personally, whether or not a place is offered.
