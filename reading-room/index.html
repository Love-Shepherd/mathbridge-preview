The Mathematics Reading Room  ·  Free  ·  By application

A quiet place to do mathematics, together.

There is a particular kind of hour that is very hard to find on your own. You sit down with mathematics, nothing is due, nobody interrupts you, and you work. One hour like that is worth more than a week of hurried homework, and almost nothing in a school timetable makes room for it.

The Mathematics Reading Room is that hour, held open every week. Students come from the IB Diploma, A Level, AP and olympiad preparation, and each one brings whatever they are working through. For most of the session we work quietly, side by side. Near the end we take one question and think it through together, out loud, the way mathematicians actually talk about mathematics.

It costs nothing, and it is not a class. There is nothing to hand in and nobody is marking you. You come with your own work, and you leave having done some of it in good company.

The session

What two hours actually looks like.

Most of the session

Quiet, independent work

You bring your own mathematics. A problem set, a past paper, a proof you cannot get past, a chapter you would like to read properly for once. Everyone works on their own thing, in the same room, which turns out to be the part that makes it possible. Cameras are optional and nobody is asked to perform.

The last stretch

One question, together

We take a single exam-style question and work it through as a group. Not a model answer read out. The real thing: what the question is asking, what we try first, why that does not work, and what we try next.

No lectures. No homework. No pressure.

Who it is for

Students who already like mathematics.

The room is for students who want more mathematics than a timetable allows. Most are working through one of these.

  • IB Diploma Mathematics, Analysis and Approaches or Applications and Interpretation
  • AS Level and A Level Mathematics and Further Mathematics
  • AP Calculus and AP Statistics
  • Olympiad preparation
  • Or any problem you are working through and would like company with

You do not need to be ahead of anyone, and you do not need to arrive with something impressive. You need to want the hour. Members are fifteen or older.
